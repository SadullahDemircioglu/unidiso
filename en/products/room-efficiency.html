Unidiso/Products/Classroom Efficiency

Make the classroom manageable

One of an institution's biggest and least visible problems is classroom use: which lecturer is in which room, when should the room open, how many students will come, how many desks and what equipment are there? When these questions go unanswered, classes run in large rooms with few students, empty rooms are heated, and the case for a new building is built on a problem that may not exist. Classroom Efficiency answers all of these on one screen, on the building floor plan.

Classroom Efficiency

What it does

Room–course–lecturer matching

Which lecturer, in which room, on which day and hour; automatic from D-plan timetable data.

Capacity fit

Expected student numbers compared with the room's desks and equipment; large rooms for small classes and small rooms for large classes are flagged.

Opening and usage hours

When each room opens, how long it is used and when it stands empty is clear.

Sensor support

Sensors in classrooms feed real usage data into the system; the gap between planned and actual use becomes visible.

Floor plan and availability map

Rooms on the building floor plan together with occupancy; the weekly availability table at a glance.

Building and campus summary

Classroom efficiency reported at building, faculty and campus level as a basis for investment decisions.

Inside the product

Scroll to move between screens.

Economic and spatial gains

Once room details are manageable, idle space and unnecessary opening hours disappear. Heating, lighting and cleaning costs fall, and because existing space is used better, the need for new classrooms is judged on data.

  • Room, lecturer, student numbers and equipment on one screen
  • Planned-versus-actual usage with sensors
  • Measurable savings in space and energy cost
  • With D-plan: efficiency findings feed the next term's plan
