Academic staff efficiency, in ratios

This product is different from A-Score: it measures internal performance, not publications and citations. How many courses does a lecturer teach, how many could they teach, how much of each course was delivered, where do they stand in internal evaluations? Academic Staff Efficiency answers these questions with data pulled from the timetable and institutional sources — based on ratios, not estimates.

Request a demo for this product
Academic Staff Efficiency

What it does

Teaching load and capacity

Courses taught compared with courses that could be taught; lecturers at full capacity and those with room are visible.

Course coverage

How much of each course's planned syllabus has been delivered, as a percentage; gaps are spotted before the term ends.

Internal evaluation criteria

The institution's evaluation criteria and weights enter the calculation; different criteria sets can be defined per faculty.

Faculty and department view

Overall evaluation summary, criteria distribution by faculty, highest- and lowest-performing lecturers.

Lecturer list

Each lecturer's teaching load, coverage rate and evaluation score in one sortable, filterable table.

Integrated and open data

Data pulled automatically from the timetable (D-plan) and the student information system; integration with any system.

Inside the product

Scroll to move between screens.

Proves performance with ratios

Whose capacity is full, where there is room, which courses fall short on coverage — deans and department heads make course-distribution and staffing decisions with data. The evaluation conversation with a lecturer rests on numbers, not argument.

  • Automatic data from the timetable and institutional sources; no manual entry
  • Teaching load, capacity and coverage rate calculated in one place
  • Comparable, provable ratios by faculty and department
  • Used with A-Score, academic and internal performance sit on the same panel
