Unidiso/Products/SERP

An ERP designed for universities, usable by everyone

When general-purpose ERP systems are adapted to a university, the result is heavy, complex screens that staff avoid. SERP took the opposite route: it was shaped inside a real university's daily operation, one process at a time. Unnecessary detail was removed; from procurement to budget, meeting management to event planning, every process runs on simple screens that anyone from the rectorate to a department secretary can use from day one.

Request a demo for this product
SERP

What it does

Procurement and supplier management

Requests, approvals, supplier records and order tracking end to end. Every step is recorded, every approval sits with the authorised role.

Budget management

Budget planning by unit and line item; spending matches budget lines and deviations are visible immediately.

Meeting management

Agenda, attendees, decisions and action items in one place. Decisions are assigned to an owner and followed until closed.

Event planning

Institutional events planned with venue, resource and calendar management, linked to budget.

Management dashboard and reporting

Summary indicators by faculty and unit at a glance. Any report, in any format, at any interval.

Open integration

Connects to the student information system, accounting, e-signature and every other external system the institution already uses.

Inside the product

Click a screen or a title below to switch between screens.

Fast to roll out, faster to adopt

Most ERP projects fail not because of the software but because staff never move onto it. Because SERP's screens are simple, the entire staff switches over after a short training and processes run through the system from the first week. A high-performance infrastructure means no waiting even in peak periods.

  • A simple, role-based interface the whole staff adopts quickly
  • Scalable infrastructure that stays fast in peak periods
  • Reports produced instantly, exactly the way management wants them
  • Two-way, open integration with existing systems
